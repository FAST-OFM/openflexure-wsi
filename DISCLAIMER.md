# Research prototype disclaimer

This software is a research prototype. It has not been clinically validated,
certified as a medical device or qualified for diagnostic use. Do not use it to
make clinical decisions.

Fast OFM is an independent project based on modified OpenFlexure software. It
is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the OpenFlexure project.
The OpenFlexure name and assets identify the upstream project and are retained
for accurate attribution.

The prototype can move motors and switch illumination hardware. Incorrect pin
assignments, current limits, travel limits or calibration values can damage the
microscope, specimen, controller or LEDs. Start from outputs disabled, verify
electrical limits independently, establish a safe local reference and keep a
physical power-removal path available.

project status and evidence boundaries are documented in the companion
`FAST-OFM/fast-ofm` release.
